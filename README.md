# Supermarket Sales Analysis 

Exploratory data analysis of 1,000 supermarket sales transactions (Jan-Mar 2019) using Python.

## Tools Used 

- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
- Jupyter Notebook
  
## Files
  
- sales_analysis.ipynb: full analysis with charts and notes
- supermarket_sales.csv: dataset (1000 rows)

## What I Did 

- Checked data quality (no duplicates, no missing values)
- Analyzed revenue by product line, branch, month, payment method, customer type, gender and hour of day
- Analyzed customer ratings by product line

## Key Findings

- Food and beverages earned the highest revenue and is also the best rated product line (7.11).
- Branch C had the highest total revenue.
- January was the best month and February the weakest.
- Cash was the top payment method (34.7%), but all three methods are close.
- Members contributed 50.8% and female customers 52.0% of revenue.
- Sales peak at 7 PM, with a second high at 1 PM.
